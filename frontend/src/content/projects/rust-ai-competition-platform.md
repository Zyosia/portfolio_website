## Overview

From February to May 2025, I developed a platform for comparing Rust programs that play
**Breakthrough**, a two-player abstract strategy game. Competing programs, typically game-playing
AIs, are plugged into the platform and play matches against each other.

## What I built

- **The game engine**: the rules of Breakthrough and the game state.
- **Match management**: running and tracking games between programs.
- **Integration of competing programs**, so that independent implementations can play
  against each other on the same engine.
